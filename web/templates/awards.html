A putilla is a player dropped at least twice in the league. Team awards count the weeks each team spent with putillas rostered; player awards rank the pickups themselves. Results are cached for a few hours; recalculate after a busy waiver day.

How the awards are computed
  • 👑 Putilla Suprema / 🥈 de Plata / 🥉 de Bronce: most total weeks with putillas on the roster (1st, 2nd, 3rd).
  • ⚡ Putilla Más Activa: most distinct putillas rostered. 💎 Putilla Fiel: fewest.
  • 🏠 El Putilla Housewife: most players added and never dropped by anyone.
  • 💰 La Putilla Escondida: best value per week (minimum 5 weeks). 🌟 Putilla MVP: highest total value (minimum 10 weeks). 🗑️ Putilla Basura: worst value per week (minimum 5 weeks, MVP excluded).
  • Value: season stats prorated to the weeks on the roster, then PTS ×0.03, REB ×0.06, AST ×0.08, STL ×0.35, BLK ×0.35, 3PM ×0.12, TO ×−0.10; ×1.08 when strong in 4 categories, ×1.15 in 5 or more. Stints under 100 prorated points do not qualify.
