  • Player Lists: All team awards show the players involved in that award.

📊 Award Criteria & Calculations

🏀 Team Awards

  • 👑 Putilla Suprema: Team with most total weeks putilla players were rostered. Counts all weeks that any putilla (dropped 2+ times) was on the team.
  • 🥈 Putilla de Plata: 2nd place in total weeks putilla players were rostered.
  • 🥉 Putilla de Bronce: 3rd place in total weeks putilla players were rostered.
  • ⚡ Putilla Más Activa: Team with most unique putilla players rostered. Measures roster churn - how many different putillas did you try?
  • 💎 Putilla Fiel: Team with fewest unique putilla players. The most loyal to their bad picks.
  • 🏠 El Putilla Housewife: Team with most players added but never dropped. These are the "keepers." Note: Deni Avdija counts here!

⭐ Player Awards

  • 💰 La Putilla Escondida (Hidden Gem): Best value per week. Minimum 5 weeks rostered. Formula: Total fantasy value ÷ Weeks rostered.
  • 🌟 Putilla MVP: Highest total fantasy value. Minimum 10 weeks rostered. Rewards the putilla who provided most value overall.
  • 🗑️ Putilla Basura (Trash): Worst value per week. Minimum 5 weeks rostered. The truly bad pickup. Note: MVP winner is excluded to avoid conflicts.

📈 Value Calculations

  • Fantasy Value: Calculated using 9-category H2H scoring:
    • Points (PTS): +1.0 per point
    • Rebounds (REB): +1.2 per rebound
    • Assists (AST): +1.5 per assist
    • Steals (STL): +3.0 per steal
    • Blocks (BLK): +3.0 per block
    • Turnovers (TO): -2.0 per turnover
    • Field Goal % (FG%): +150 if above league average, -100 if below
    • Free Throw % (FT%): +60 if above league average, -40 if below
    • 3-Pointers Made (3PM): +3.0 per three
  • Prorated Stats: Player stats are prorated based on weeks they were actually on your roster, not full season stats.
  • "Weeks" or "Games Started": Both terms mean the same thing - the number of weeks a player was on a team's active roster.
